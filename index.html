Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about admissions, approvals, hostel living, or scholarships? Find quick answers here or reach out to our counseling desk.

For B.Tech, applicants must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with at least 45% aggregate (40% for reserved categories). JEE Main / CUET scores are preferred. For MBA, a recognized Bachelor’s degree in any discipline with minimum 50% is required.

Yes. The university provides separate, secure on-campus multi-story hostels for both male and female students with 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, study lounges, and hygienic multi-cuisine dining messes.

RKDF University awards substantial merit-based scholarships based on 10+2 / graduation scores, national entrance percentiles, sports achievements (national/state representation), and government post-matric welfare schemes for SC/ST/OBC students.

The centralized Training and Placement Cell operates year-round, delivering personality development, aptitude training, coding bootcamps, and mock interviews. RKDF organizes annual State-Level Mega Open Campus Drives bringing over 150+ leading MNCs directly to the campus.
